| Recital 5 | Recital |  | In order to ensure transparency and trustworthiness of wallet-relying parties towards wallet users, the protocols and interfaces used by the wallet solutions should provide wallet users with a reliable mechanism to authenticate wallet-relying parties and other wallet units.
