# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2162 — accreditation of conformity assessment bodies and assessment of qualified trust service providers

## Executive summary

This act sets the conditions under which **conformity assessment bodies (CABs)** are accredited, the requirements for the **conformity assessment schemes**, the **conformity assessment report** specifications and the **accreditation certificate** contents used to assess **qualified trust service providers (QTSPs)** and the qualified trust services they provide. Its addressees are **national accreditation bodies (NABs), CABs, scheme owners and QTSPs**, not an application publisher. Autogram is a desktop signing/validation application distributed to end users; it is not a CAB, not a NAB, not a scheme owner, not a QTSP, and it operates no hosted qualified trust service. On the established facts, almost every operative duty in this act is therefore **None / Ignored** for this publisher.

The relevance that remains is **contextual or conditional**. The act adopts the same ETSI standards that underpin Autogram's validation function — in particular the trusted-list standard **ETSI TS 119 612 v2.4.1** in Annex II and, for the qualified validation service, **ETSI EN 319 102-1, ETSI TS 119 102-2 and ETSI TS 119 172-4**, which the parallel act 2025/1945 adapts. Autogram implements signature validation by delegating to SD-DSS **6.5** and loads the EU trusted list, so these rows are **Indirect** or **Conditional**, but that is interoperability context, not a duty of the publisher. Where the app could sit inside a QTSP's assessed service as a third party or component, the scheme and report provisions become **Conditional / Unknown**; whether Autogram is used in providing an eID/trust service is itself **Unknown** and is not assumed either way.

Inspected source establishes DSS-based creation and validation, EU-LOTL loading with eight default countries, configurable online timestamp sources and cautious qualification presentation. It does **not** establish any accreditation, conformity assessment scheme, certificate of conformity, or conformity assessment report; those are outputs of CABs about QTSPs. Overall fulfilment of the act by this publisher is **not applicable**; conditional technical gaps are **Unknown**, and missing evidence is not a statutory violation. Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. Adopted **27 October 2025**, published in the OJ **28 October 2025**, effective **17 November 2025**; grandfathering until **17 May 2027**.
