# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2164 — trusted-list template update

## Executive summary

This Decision amends Annex I, Chapter II of [Decision 2015/1505](eu-dec_impl-2015-1505.md). It replaces the trusted-list standard reference with **ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08)**, clarifies interpretation rules and specifies safer whole-document XML signature transforms. That standard's clause 5.3.1 requires trusted-list format identifier **6 (TLv6)**. The amended rules apply from **29 April 2026**; entry into force was **17 November 2025**.

Member States and their trusted-list publishers, not Autogram's software publisher, must publish the prescribed lists. List interpretation and authentic processing are **indirect interoperability requirements** for Autogram. No separate statutory vendor filing or release deadline follows from this Decision. This does not remove validation-system duties arising under eIDAS itself.

The existing report records successful live validation of the **EU LOTL and Slovak TLv6 on 3 October 2026 only**. This review did not repeat that test. Other countries, historical qualification, every qualifier, remote-QSCD management and rejection of malformed list signatures remain **Unknown**. Eight countries are still selected by default. Source code and one positive live test are not ETSI conformity or EU-wide QES-validation certification.

## Evidence key

References below identify inspected application code or the exact outstanding evidence, not presumed compliance:

- **E1 — list processing:** [`SignatureValidator.initialize`, lines 91–133](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L91-L133), configures DSS LOTL, country filtering, pivot support, cached online/offline loaders and trusted certificate source. `getJournalCertificateSource`, lines 135–142, loads the LOTL keystore; `getTrustedListStatus`, lines 273–301, checks valid LOTL and every selected country. These are delegation/configuration evidence, not a clause-by-clause DSS audit.
- **E2 — limited live result:** `TrustedListLiveSmokeTest.validatesCurrentSlovakTrustedListThroughEuLotl`, lines 11–25, opts into network testing and selects **SK only**. The historical passing result and SK XML identifier 6 are recorded in the [3 October evidence table](../eidas-2-gap-matrix.md#1-evidence-obtained). The test does not itself assert XML version 6 or validate a signed user document. No new execution or independently retained run log was obtained here.
- **E3 — country scope:** [`UserSettings.DEFAULT_TRUSTED_LIST`, line 43](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/UserSettings.java#L43) is `SK,CZ,AT,PL,HU,BE,NL,ES`.
- **P — publisher role:** confirmed scope is local software, not a national trusted-list operator or QTSP. No Member-State publication mandate exists in the reviewed facts. National publication and supervisory records were not audited.
- **Q — interpretation gap:** need provenance-verified list/certificate/signature fixtures and expected qualification outcomes at the relevant time for the particular row, plus a DSS/application requirement mapping. E1/E2 do not establish that outcome.
- **S — list-signature gap:** need authenticated TLv6 snapshots and negative tests for reference scope, transforms, tampering, wrapping, pivot rotation and stale/offline input. The live positive E2 does not prove malicious-input rejection or every list's signature profile.

In the tables, **Ignored** means contextual or out of scope, never disregarding an applicable duty. **Unknown** on indirect rules means unproved input handling, not an established statutory breach. Unless noted otherwise, all annex replacements apply **29 April 2026**. Provision paths distinguish the Annex's amendment point (2) from its replacement section's own URI points (1) and (2). Unnumbered paragraphs and dash entries receive descriptive labels for traceability.
