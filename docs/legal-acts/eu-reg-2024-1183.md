# Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 — European Digital Identity Framework (eIDAS amendment)

## Executive summary

This act substantially amends [Regulation (EU) No 910/2014](eu-reg-2014-910.md), rather than imposing a general wallet-implementation deadline on desktop signing software. Autogram's local validation system is directly relevant to inserted **Article 32a(1)–(2)** and, through Article 40a, advanced seals based on qualified certificates. Existing Article 32(1)–(2) continues to govern qualified-signature validation: the amendment adds a standards-based presumption, not an exemption for non-QTSP developers. Overall correctness remains **Unknown**; DSS integration and qualification badges do not prove all legal validation conditions.

**Article 15 is Conditional / Unknown**, not automatically inapplicable: it expressly includes end-user products used in providing electronic identification/trust services. Establishing the product's actual use and assessing accessible language and operation are outstanding. Wallet-provider, wallet-relying-party, certificate-issuer and trust-service-provider duties are not duties of this publisher under the confirmed local-software scope. Paid support alone does not establish a trust service. The definitions and any future service contracts still need role review.

Assessment: **8 October 2026**, repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. The amendment entered into force **20 May 2024**. Wallet deployment/acceptance periods run from specified implementing acts; dates addressed to the Commission are not Autogram release deadlines. Legacy-device, certificate and remote-management transitions have separate cutoffs. This is an engineering/legal reading register, **not legal advice, certification or an audit of a released binary**.

- **B**: [`SignatureBadgeFactory.java`](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/gui/SignatureBadgeFactory.java), `createBadgeFromQualification` lines 68–94 and `createCombinedBadgeFromQualification` lines 111–147: QESIG/ADESIG_QC/QESEAL/ADESEAL_QC and indeterminate presentation. Distinct labels are evidence of presentation, not all validation conditions.
- **Scope**: the confirmed facts in [README](README.md): local desktop/API/CLI publisher, no hosted document-processing service and no QTSP status. Eight default trusted-list countries and a reported SK TLv6/LOTL smoke test do not establish all-country or historical correctness. No new tests were run for this document.
- **Role evidence**: a future provider/wallet role would require actual service contracts, Member State mandate/recognition, registration, qualification decisions, operational architecture and conformity reports. No such role is established here.
- **A11y evidence**: intended-use/distribution and customer deployment records identifying an end-user product used in providing eID/trust services; keyboard/screen-reader, plain-language, disability-user and applicable Directive 2019/882 assessment evidence. None establishes overall Article 15 fulfilment here.

Parent provisions introducing lists are structural headings, not duplicate obligations; each operative child has a separate row. An unnumbered subparagraph is identified within its numbered paragraph. “Ignored” always means contextual or outside the confirmed scope, never deliberate breach. Commission/Member State rows are contextual unless expressly noted.

**How enumeration was checked.** The 78 recitals were parsed between `Whereas:` and the enacting formula. The Article 1 amendment blocks were verified against the official numbering: 53 sequential top-level points, from `(1)` "Article 1 is replaced" to `(53)` "new Annexes V, VI and VII are added". Definition insertions inside block `(3)` (points `(4)`–`(57)` of base Article 3) are assessed in the [base document](eu-reg-2014-910.md) and not double-counted here as amendment blocks. Every recital and amendment-map row links to the authoritative text at the affected provision.

**What is not evidenced here.** (1) Correct validation outcomes for real qualified signatures/seals; only source structure and presentation were inspected. (2) Whether any release is an end-user product used in providing an eID/trust service, which decides conditional Article 15 (assessed in the base document). (3) Organisational facts that would establish a wallet, relying-party or trust-service role; none is established. (4) No exhaustive later corrigenda/amendment search beyond the 9 April 2025 corrigendum is claimed.

**Relation to the base document.** Regulation 2024/1183 does not create a second, parallel set of duties; it rewrites and adds provisions of Regulation 910/2014. This document therefore records the **legislative operations** and cross-links the resulting obligations, which are enumerated at article, paragraph and letter level in [eu-reg-2014-910.md](eu-reg-2014-910.md). Where the two overlap, the base document's per-provision tables are authoritative for the consolidated result.
