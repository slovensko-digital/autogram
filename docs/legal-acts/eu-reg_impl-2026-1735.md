# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1735 — attribute-attestation standards update

**Register:** [Autogram legal-act register](README.md) · **Assessment date:** 8 October 2026 · **Repository baseline:** `5ca91d5c`. **Amends:** [2025/1569](eu-reg_impl-2025-1569.md).

## Executive summary

**What this act is.** An amending regulation updating the **applicable standards and specifications** of Implementing Regulation 2025/1569 on qualified electronic attestations of attributes, including Article 3(2) on providers and Article 4 and Article 9. Adopted **15 July 2026**, published **22 July 2026**.

**Relevance to Autogram: None (context).** Autogram does not issue electronic attestations of attributes. This is a register entry recording the amendment operation and cross-linking the amended act.

**Dates.** Adopted 15 Jul 2026; publication 22 Jul 2026; entry into force per Article 2, with **Article 1(3) applying from 1 January 2027**.
