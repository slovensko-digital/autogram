# Commission Implementing Decision 2014/148/EU — cross-border signed authority documents

## Executive summary

This **Commission Implementing Decision**, not a regulation or a standalone general signature-format law, amends Decision 2011/130/EU under the Services Directive. It requires **Member States** to provide technical processing of specified electronically signed documents issued by other Member States' competent authorities and submitted through Points of Single Contact. Its relevance to Autogram desktop, local API and CLI is **Indirect**: public-service processing/recognition duties are not independent software-publisher obligations or release deadlines.

The inspected creation code selects XAdES, CAdES, PAdES and associated containers, but does not establish conformity with the exact historical ETSI editions or acceptance of all foreign-authority inputs. That interoperability remains **Unknown**, not an established statutory failure. Application began **1 December 2014**; entry into force was **18 March 2014**, on notification. EUR-Lex search metadata identifies the act as **in force**; it must not be labelled EU-repealed because the UK archive says “revoked”. An English corrigendum corrects the publication heading, not signature requirements.

Assessment: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline: **5ca91d5c**. This is a traceable reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit. Shared scope and vocabulary: [README](README.md).

### Inspected application evidence

- **E1:** [`SigningJob.createSignatureService`, lines 130–149](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java#L130-L149) dispatches to XAdES/CAdES/PAdES services and ASiC-with-XAdES/CAdES services. This proves selection paths, not complete input-processing support or standard conformity.
- **E2:** [`DssSigningParametersFactory`, lines 14–20 and 29–97](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/DssSigningParametersFactory.java#L14-L97) sets signature levels, packaging, container type and profile-related switches. Configuration alone does not prove conformity with the annex's exact editions or all conformance levels.
- Missing **E3:** a version-specific conformity matrix, signed sample corpus for the four cited specifications, receiving Point-of-Single-Contact integration results, and foreign-authority signature-validation outcomes. No public-service processing deployment or responsibility of this publisher is established.

## Coverage and limitations

- **Sources obtained:** complete adopted English HTML and XML from the official UK archive; the **3-page original English OJ PDF**, pp. 7–9; and the **1-page English corrigendum PDF**, OJ L 95, p. 69, directly from the EU Publications Office. The XML includes archived EU relationship/date metadata; it is not a live 2026 EU status feed.
- **Enumeration:** **9 recitals; 3 articles; 2 numbered amendment points; 1 inserted numbered base-act paragraph; 3 unnumbered article passages** (Article 1 introduction and Articles 2 and 3); **1 annex with 2 unnumbered paragraphs and 4 individual specification entries; 1 English corrigendum correction block**. There are **22 assessment rows**: 9 recital rows, 6 article/amendment rows, 6 annex rows and 1 correction row. Parent structural headings are not extra duties; the correction's “for/read” lines constitute one replacement block. There are no lettered subpoints or other numbered annex points.
- The original PDF's two-column body was checked against the adopted HTML to avoid extraction-order omissions. The annex's four profile/version entries and associated footnote URLs were compared individually. The seven bibliographic footnotes across body/annex are references, not additional operative provisions. Title, legal-basis citations, adoption formula and signature block were inspected and reflected in source information rather than treated as implementation requirements.
- **No original English article, recital or annex entry was omitted.** English coverage also includes the retrieved R(01) heading correction. R(02) and R(03) texts were unavailable; archived metadata identifies them as Czech-only and Slovak-only respectively. No claim is made to cover their language-specific corrections or to have completed live post-archive EU amendment verification. Direct EUR-Lex text and consolidation downloads remained unavailable.
- **Zero of the four ETSI standards were inspected.** They are separately linked works, not reproduced or silently treated as part of the decision's obtained full text. Exact-edition conformity, all-level processing, long-term validity outcomes and public-service acceptance remain unproved.
- Only the cited code paths were inspected; no builds, tests, released binaries, receiving public services, support contracts or organisational compliance documents were audited. The current eIDAS public-body format regime requires a separate assessment; this Services Directive decision cannot establish a general Autogram recognition obligation or today's eIDAS transition dates.
