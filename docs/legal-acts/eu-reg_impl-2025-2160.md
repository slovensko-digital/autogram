# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2160 — risk management by non-qualified trust-service providers

## Executive summary

This act lays down the reference standards, specifications and procedures for the management of risks to the provision of **non-qualified trust services**, complementing the cybersecurity framework of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 and implementing the presumption of compliance in eIDAS Article 19a(2). It is addressed to **providers of non-qualified trust services**. The publisher is **not a qualified trust-service provider** and operates **no hosted trust service**: Autogram is a local desktop application ([README.md](../../README.md#L4)) that signs with the user's own signing key on the user's machine ([SigningJob.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java#L86-L109)). Paid support above cost recovery does **not** by itself prove that the organisation operates a non-qualified trust service, so the operative provider obligations below are assessed as **Conditional / Unknown**, not established direct duties. The pivotal unresolved fact is the organisation's role: whether it actually provides a non-qualified trust service.

The act has three substantive articles: Article 2 (risk-management policies and their minimum content, approval and procedures), Article 3 (identification, documentation and evaluation of risks) and Article 4 (risk-treatment measures, identity verification, record retention, review and residual-risk approval). Article 1 points to a single Annex that imports the requirements of specified clauses of **ETSI EN 319 401 V3.1.1 (2024-06)** — clauses **5, 6, 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 7.4 and 7.6**. The external standard is a separate work, not reproduced by the act, and was **not inspected** here.

No provider-wide risk-management policy, risk register, risk-treatment plan, management-body approval, asset classification, residual-risk acceptance or legislative-monitoring procedure can be evidenced in this repository; [RELEASE.md](../../RELEASE.md#L5-L10) is a manual release/test checklist, not such a policy. Missing evidence is **not** proof of a statutory violation, and the existence of organisational documents elsewhere is not excluded.

Adopted **27 October 2025**, published **28 October 2025**, in force **17 November 2025** (twentieth day following publication). Assessment/source access **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. This is a traceable reading aid, **not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit**.

## Legend and provision links

**Relevance:** Direct · Conditional · Indirect · None. **Status:** Done · Not done · Unknown · Ignored (context or out of the confirmed scope; never "knowingly disregarded"). Relevance and status are independent.

Every provision row links to the official text at that unit — articles `#art_1`–`#art_5`, recitals `#rct_1`–`#rct_9` and the Annex `#anx_1` on the [official ELI page](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/2160/oj/eng). Preamble rows link to the act as a whole.

`Conditional` below means **if the organisation is, or becomes, a provider of a non-qualified trust service** within eIDAS Article 3, point (19), read with Article 19a. That condition is unresolved. Publishing or supporting a local application, even with paid support, is not by itself proof of operating a non-qualified trust service. The act addresses providers; it is not an autonomous desktop-software deadline.
