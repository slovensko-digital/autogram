# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1568 — cooperation and peer review of eID schemes

**Register:** [Autogram legal-act register](README.md) · **Assessment date:** 8 October 2026 · **Repository baseline:** `5ca91d5c` · **Scope:** Autogram desktop application, locally hosted API and CLI. **Replaces:** [Decision (EU) 2015/296](eu-dec_impl-2015-296.md).

## Executive summary

**What this act is.** Regulation 2025/1568 lays down the procedural arrangements for **cooperation between Member States on electronic identification** and for the **peer review of electronic identification schemes**, under eIDAS Article 12(5)–(6) as amended by [Regulation 2024/1183](eu-reg-2024-1183.md). It replaces Decision 2015/296.

**Who it addresses.** Every operative provision addresses **Member States**, their identification-scheme authorities, the **European Digital Identity Cooperation Group** and the **Commission**. There is no obligation on an independent signing/validation application.

**Relevance to Autogram: None (context).** Autogram does not operate an eID scheme, does not issue eID means, is not a member of the Cooperation Group and does not carry out peer reviews. The act is registered for completeness because it sits in the eIDAS-2.0 family and because the schemes it reviews are the ones a validator ultimately relies on. The only indirect touchpoint is that a future wallet/eID integration would depend on notified schemes reviewed under this procedure; none exists today.

**Dates.** Adopted **29 July 2025**; published in the OJ; entry into force on the twentieth day after publication (Article 8). No vendor deadline follows.

## Coverage and limitations

**Text obtained.** Complete English XHTML of Regulation 2025/1568 from the Publications Office/Cellar, including preamble, **all 13 recitals**, **Articles 1–8** with their numbered paragraphs, letters and points, and **Annexes I–IV**. The act is the original published version; it repeals Decision 2015/296.

**Enumeration check.** Article headings were extracted as standalone lines (8 articles); paragraph and letter markers were read from the extracted text; Annex I points (1)–(4) and their letters, Annex II points 1–3, Annex III's requirement rows (Articles 4–11 of 2015/1501) and Annex IV's template were enumerated. Where a letter's detailed text is a template placeholder, the row records the requirement without reproducing the full template.

**What is not evidenced and why it does not matter here.** No Autogram code implements this act, and none is expected to: the act creates no application or publisher duty. Its provisions are therefore **None / Ignored** under the confirmed scope, with recital (11) flagged **Indirect/Unknown** only because it points to data-protection rules assessed in the [GDPR document](eu-reg-2016-679.md). The referenced external acts (2015/1501, 2015/1502) are separate works with their own register documents.

**Status rationale.** An entry marked **Ignored** here means "outside the confirmed scope of a local signing/validation application", not a deliberate disregard. If Autogram ever integrates a notified eID scheme or the EUDI Wallet as a relying application, this act becomes context for the schemes it consumes, not an obligation on Autogram itself.
