# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2981 — certification of European Digital Identity Wallets

## Executive summary

This act lays down rules for applying Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS) as regards the **certification of European Digital Identity Wallets** ("wallets"). It obliges Member States to establish **national certification schemes**, scheme owners to develop and maintain them, wallet providers to submit wallet solutions for evaluation, and certification bodies to evaluate and certify them. Its addressees are public authorities, notified/private certification bodies and wallet providers. It is not an act about ordinary document signing, signature creation devices or signature validation.

Under the confirmed scope, Autogram is a desktop application that signs and verifies documents using external PKCS#11 tokens/cards, a local HTTP API and a CLI ([`README.md`, lines 4–6](../../README.md#L4-L6)). It is **not a wallet solution, wallet provider, wallet secure cryptographic application (WSCA), wallet secure cryptographic device (WSCD), scheme owner, certification body or Member State**, holds no wallet certification and contains no wallet, PID or attestation code. The individual requirements below are therefore scoped out, not reported as completed or breached. Provisions that would bear on Autogram only if its code were supplied as part of a certified wallet solution are marked **Conditional** with the condition stated; a future wallet role would need a fresh assessment.

The original act was adopted on **28 November 2024**, published on **4 December 2024**, and entered into force on the twentieth day following publication, i.e. **24 December 2024** (Art. 22). No consolidation, amending act or corrigendum for 2024/2981 was found in the official records inspected, so this document assesses the original OJ text. The cited ETSI/EN ISO/IEC standards and the cross-referenced implementing regulations (2024/2977, 2024/2979, 2024/2982) are separate works and were not inspected. This is a traceable engineering/legal reading aid, **not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit**.
