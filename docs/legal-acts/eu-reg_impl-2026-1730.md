# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1730 — wallet-relying-party registration standards update

**Register:** [Autogram legal-act register](README.md) · **Assessment date:** 8 October 2026 · **Repository baseline:** `5ca91d5c`. **Amends:** [2025/848](eu-reg_impl-2025-848.md).

## Executive summary

**What this act is.** An amending regulation updating the **applicable standards and specifications** of Implementing Regulation 2025/848 (registration of wallet-relying parties), including data requested by wallet-relying parties. Adopted **15 July 2026**, published **22 July 2026**.

**Relevance to Autogram: None (context).** Autogram is not a wallet or wallet-relying party. This is a register entry recording the amendment operation and cross-linking the amended act.

**Dates.** Adopted 15 Jul 2026; publication 22 Jul 2026; entry into force per Article 2.
