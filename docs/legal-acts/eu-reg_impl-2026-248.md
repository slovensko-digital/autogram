# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/248 — public-sector recognition of signature and seal formats

## Executive summary

This act **exists**: it was adopted on **2 February 2026**, published on **3 February 2026**, and entered into force on **23 February 2026**. It replaces [Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/1506](eu-dec_impl-2015-1506.md). It primarily imposes recognition and alternative-validation duties on **Member States in the public-sector online-service context**, not a general implementation deadline on the independent publisher of Autogram.

Its relevance to Autogram is **Indirect** interoperability context. The inspected creation path supports XAdES, CAdES, PAdES and associated ASiC containers, but **not JAdES**. These observations do not establish conformity to the exact ETSI versions in either annex; that remains **Unknown**. No breach of a vendor duty is established by the missing JAdES creation path. Member-State alternative validation services are not services operated by this organisation under the confirmed scope.

The Annex I recognition duties in Articles 1(1) and 3(1) apply from **23 February 2027**. Articles 1(2) and 3(2) preserve recognition of Annex II formats for signatures/seals **created before 23 February 2028**. That date is a **creation cutoff**, not an expiry of recognition for qualifying earlier signatures/seals. Recital 8 explains recognition for as long as validation is required by EU or national law; no blanket recognition sunset for those earlier objects appears in the operative text.

## Coverage and limitations

- **Obtained official text:** complete English published OJ XHTML from the EU Publications Office, including preamble/legal bases, **all 11 recitals**, **Articles 1–6**, closing formulas, **both annexes**, and all **7 main-text, 6 Annex I and 4 Annex II source footnotes**. The PDF is linked but was not independently downloaded/visually audited; direct EUR-Lex access was challenged. No operative text or annex was unavailable in the obtained XHTML.
- **Enumeration check:** read the full extracted official text through the Annex II footnotes and terminal ELI, and checked the XHTML structure. Tables separately identify Articles 1(1)/(2), 3(1)/(2); for each of Articles 2 and 4, paragraphs 1–3, paragraph 1 letters (a)/(b), paragraph 2 letters (a)–(e), and all **nine unnumbered dash conditions** under (e); both Article 5 unnumbered paragraphs; both Article 6 unnumbered paragraphs and the closing binding formula. Paragraph/letter introductions are retained rather than used to hide their subconditions. Annex I has all four signature/seal profiles, both container specifications, the JAdES replacement instruction and all three replacement paragraphs; Annex II has all three signature/seal profiles and its container profile, with each profile's exceptions/levels retained.
- **Non-duty text:** article/annex headings, replacement-clause title, legal bases, signatory/date formula and bibliographic footnotes are covered as structure/source metadata, not assigned invented software obligations. Cross-links to 2015/1506 do not replace any provision rows. That sibling document was not present when this assessment began; the cross-link is intentional for the shared register and this task does not create/edit it.
- **External standards:** the annex citations and official links were inspected **as printed in the act**. The **ten referenced ETSI documents were not downloaded or read clause by clause**, and RFC 7515 was not independently inspected. No claim is made to cover their full contents; only the annex's own explicit JAdES replacement is assessed paragraph by paragraph. Exact current/legacy format conformity, adapted JAdES validation, LT handling and the Part 2 clause 4.3.1 additional-container option remain **Unknown**.
- **Evidence limits:** source and test code inspected, baseline comparison performed; no builds, tests, output generation, public-service acceptance tests or fresh live trusted-list tests run. E5 checks response behaviour; E6 checks mocked presentation. Neither certifies conformity or all legal qualification outcomes. No official Member-State alternative validation offer, free online service, complete method specifications, embedded method indication or seal-specific conformance corpus was obtained.
- **Outstanding facts:** receiving public-sector service requirements, TSP establishment, historical certificate/QTSP/device evidence, all relevant country trusted lists, exact format/level/edition fixtures, alternative-method operational/accessibility evidence, organisation processing roles and any deployment on behalf of a public-sector body. Exact conformance is **Unknown**, not “Done” by dependency or format name, and not “Not done” as an inapplicable vendor statutory duty.
