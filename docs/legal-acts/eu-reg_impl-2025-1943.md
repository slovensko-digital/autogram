# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1943 — reference standards for qualified certificates for electronic signatures and seals

## Executive summary

This act adapts the standards supporting the eIDAS presumption of compliance for the **issuance of qualified certificates for electronic signatures** (Annex I, under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 Article 28(6)) and **qualified certificates for electronic seals** (Annex II, under Article 38(6), as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183). Its addressees are qualified trust service providers (QTSPs) and the supervisory bodies that assess them; it does not impose an issuance duty on this publisher.

The publisher is **not a certificate issuer and not a QTSP**. Autogram receives a qualified certificate as *input* — read from a signing token and used as the signing certificate, and read from signed documents during signature validation. That input role makes the certificate-profile standards referenced by the annexes **Indirect** interoperability criteria, not a direct publisher obligation. The operative issuance requirements (identity validation, personnel, key management, network security, termination, CPS/CP content) are **None / Ignored** for this publisher.

Inspected source establishes that Autogram reads and filters certificates by expiry, passes the signing certificate and chain to DSS, validates signed documents through the EU LOTL and selected national trusted lists, and presents qualification badges cautiously. It does **not** establish any EN 319 411-2 / 412-x issuance-policy implementation or certificate-profile / QCStatement inspection; the profile and algorithm rows are therefore marked **Indirect / Unknown** with the missing evidence named. Overall fulfilment of this act by the publisher is not asserted; missing proof is not a demonstrated statutory violation.

Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. Adopted **29 September 2025**, published **30 September 2025**, effective **20 October 2025**.
