# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2530 — requirements for qualified trust service providers providing qualified trust services

## Executive summary

This act lays down the requirements for **qualified trust-service providers (QTSPs)** providing qualified trust services: notification of significant changes to the supervisory body (Article 1), a risk-management framework (Article 2), a termination plan (Article 3) and the list of reference standards and specifications whose implementation supports the eIDAS **Article 24(5) presumption of compliance** (Article 4 and the Annex). Its addressees are QTSPs and the Commission. The organisation publishing Autogram is **not a qualified trust-service provider** and operates no hosted service receiving users' documents. On that established scope most operative provisions are **None / Ignored**, with a limited number of **Indirect** rows where Autogram concretely exercises the same subject matter on the client side — local token signing, DSS validation, trusted-list/CRL/OCSP retrieval, external timestamping and local validation reports.

Conditionality attaches only where an unresolved role or deployment could change the conclusion: **Recital (9)** personal-data processing, and the possibility of Autogram being supplied into a qualified service as a "trustworthy system or product" under Article 24(2), points (e)–(f), referenced by Article 1(1)(b). No row is marked **Done** or **Not done**: this act does not create a proven statutory duty on this publisher, and the inspected source does not prove complete QTSP-side fulfilment either. The Annex's cross-referenced standards (Implementing Regulations 2025/1943, 2025/1942, 2025/1946, 2025/1929, 2025/1944, 2025/1567, 2025/2532, 2025/1569, 2025/2531) are **separate works**, not reproduced or inspected here.

Inspected source establishes a client-side signing/validation application: token-held keys via DSS ([SigningJob.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java), [SigningKey.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningKey.java)), trusted-list and revocation configuration ([SignatureValidator.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java)), external TSA use ([UserSettings.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/UserSettings.java)) and local simple reports ([ValidationReports.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/ValidationReports.java)). None of that makes the publisher a QTSP. Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. Adopted **16 December 2025**, published **17 December 2025**, in force **6 January 2026**.

## Evidence key

All evidence below was inspected on 8 October 2026. Source inspection is not a released-binary audit or proof that tests pass; no builds or tests were run. The publisher's non-QTSP status and local desktop/API/CLI scope are the register's confirmed facts.

- **F — scope fact:** [README.md, lines 4–6](../../README.md#L4-L6): a multi-platform desktop JavaFX application for signing/verifying documents under eIDAS, with a local HTTP API and CLI. The application binds a local API server, not a hosted document-receiving service ([AppStarter.java, lines 13–14](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/AppStarter.java#L13-L14)). These facts establish the reviewed boundary, not an exhaustive negative proof about every release or contract.
- **S — signing path:** [SigningJob.java, `signWithKeyAndRespond`, lines 86–109](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java#L86-L109) computes `dataToSign` through DSS, signs via the selected key and returns a signed document; [SigningKey.java, `sign`, lines 24–29](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningKey.java#L24-L29) delegates to the token, and lines 45–47 expose the certificate chain. [DssSigningParametersFactory.java, lines 14–27](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/DssSigningParametersFactory.java#L14-L27) builds XAdES/CAdES/PAdES parameters; [SigningParameters.java, lines 57–58 and 92–94](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningParameters.java#L57-L58) default the digest to **SHA-256**. Keys are held in the user's token/keystore, not by the publisher.
- **V — validation and trusted lists:** [SignatureValidator.java, `initialize`, lines 91–133](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L91-L133) configures the EU LOTL, country filtering and online CRL/OCSP sources (lines 127–130); `validate`, lines 76–81, delegates to DSS `validateDocument()`. [TrustedListStatus, lines 273–301](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L273-L301) requires a valid LOTL and all selected countries. This is client-side validation, not a qualified validation service.
- **T — timestamps:** [UserSettings.java, `setTsaServer`, lines 357–375](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/UserSettings.java#L357-L375) builds a composite `OnlineTSPSource`; [SigningJob.java, lines 146–147](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java#L146-L147) attaches it only for `BASELINE_T`. [SignatureBadgeFactory.java, lines 97–107](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/gui/SignatureBadgeFactory.java#L97-L107) distinguishes QTSA/TSA/unknown timestamp qualification. Autogram consumes an external timestamp; it does not issue qualified timestamps.
- **R — local reports:** [ValidationReports.java, `DocumentReport`, lines 14–26 and `getSignatures`, lines 114–119](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/ValidationReports.java#L14-L26) retain DSS reports per document/signature; [SignatureValidator.java, `getSignatureValidationReport`, lines 145–163](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L145-L163) collects them. These are local presentation artefacts, not QTSP records.
- **A — local API/CLI:** [AppStarter.java, lines 11–30](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/AppStarter.java#L11-L30) exposes CLI options including `--tsa-server`; the API is served locally via `com.sun.net.httpserver` ([ServerResponder.java, lines 13–35](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/server/ServerResponder.java#L13-L35)).
