# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/650 — QSCD security-assessment standards

## Executive summary

This Decision identifies standards for **qualified signature/seal creation device (QSCD) certification**, not certification of Autogram as a signature-creation application. Its relevance to the local desktop/API/CLI application is principally **Indirect**: the status and certified configuration of the actual device matter to any qualified-signature claim. A certified card does **not** certify the application, its preview, or every signature it produces.

Inspected source delegates signing to a token and separately constructs document previews and signature containers. It does not establish device certification, certified operating conditions, or preview-to-signed-content equivalence. Overall device/conformity evidence is **Unknown**. Recital 7's exclusion of signature-creation applications from device certification does **not** waive eIDAS validation-system duties (Articles 32(2), 32a(2), and corresponding seal provisions). eIDAS Article 15 remains **Conditional** where Autogram is an end-user product used in providing electronic identification or trust services; that deployment fact is unresolved, not disproved by the publisher's non-QTSP status.

Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. Effective date: **16 May 2016**. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit; apply the shared facts and vocabulary in [README.md](README.md).

## Inspected repository evidence

- **E1 — local token boundary:** [`PKCS11TokenDriver.createToken`, lines 11–18](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/drivers/PKCS11TokenDriver.java#L11-L18) constructs a native PKCS#11 token connection. [`SigningKey.sign`, lines 15–29](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningKey.java#L15-L29) delegates the to-be-signed data to `token.sign`, with SHA-256 fallback. Neither establishes the token's certified model/configuration or key-length suitability.
- **E2 — preview and signing boundary:** [`SigningJob.initializeVisualizations` and `signWithKeyAndRespond`, lines 79–108](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java#L79-L108) construct visualizations separately from DSS `getDataToSign`/`signDocument` paths, including multi-document containers. Need fixtures proving the displayed document/content corresponds to what is signed, including transformations and container extraction; source inspection alone does not prove this.
- **E3 — validation exists independently of device certification:** [`SignatureValidator.initialize`, lines 91–130](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L91-L130) configures selected-country LOTL/trusted lists, CRL and OCSP; [`getSignatureValidationReport`, lines 145–162](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java#L145-L162) obtains per-document reports. These measures do not prove complete qualified-signature/seal validation or Article 15 accessibility.
- **Missing device evidence:** actual card/device inventory, designation/certification records, certificate status and scope, security targets/protection profiles, supported firmware/configurations, operational restrictions and their mapping to each claimed QES/seal. No such dossier was obtained.
- **Missing conditional deployment evidence:** contracts and architecture showing whether this product is used to provide eID/trust services, and an accessibility assessment for persons with disabilities if Article 15 applies. No build, test execution, device audit or independent standard-conformity assessment was performed.

## Coverage and limitations

- **Complete adopted legal text obtained:** English EU OJ facsimile, pp. 40–42, including the recital 7 continuation across pages 40/41, the full Article 1(2) opening condition and Commission-notification ending, and all of the Annex on p. 42. Tables enumerate **10 recitals**, **4 operative units** (Article 1(1), 1(2), Article 2's sole unnumbered paragraph, Article 3's sole unnumbered paragraph), and **12 Annex dash entries** (3 top-level entries and 9 nested entries). Two legal-basis paragraphs are additionally identified. No operative numbered paragraph, lettered subpoint or Annex entry in that facsimile is omitted; none of the operative units contains lettered subpoints. The adoption/signature block and two bibliographic footnotes were read and used for metadata/source relationships, not misclassified as duties.
- Coverage was checked against the **three actual PDF pages**, not inferred from the existing report or UK-amended HTML. Complete legal text is linked rather than reproduced. The table summaries are English paraphrases, not replacement authoritative text.
- **Current-version limitation:** current EUR-Lex full relationship/consolidation data could not be retrieved. Search-index metadata indicates in-force status and no end date; targeted searches did not identify an EU amendment/corrigendum. This does not establish exhaustive absence of changes as of 8 October 2026. Verification of the complete EUR-Lex “Relationship between documents” record remains outstanding before claiming complete current-law coverage. UK retained-law changes are deliberately excluded.
- **Standards limitation:** no full ISO/IEC 15408, ISO/IEC 18045 or CEN EN 419211 standard was obtained/inspected, and no referenced ETSI work was inspected. This Decision's Annex names ISO/CEN standards, not ETSI standards. Their clauses, security assurance levels, implementation tests and device-specific profile selection are separate evidence, not silently imported legal provisions.
- **Product/organisation limitation:** source inspection establishes E1–E3 only. No card certification dossier, deployed-device audit, released-binary audit, preview-equivalence regression run, cryptographic-policy review, validation-conformance test suite, Article 15 deployment determination or accessibility audit was obtained. Missing evidence is not a statutory violation. Certification exclusion cannot resolve validation duties or conditional accessibility obligations.
