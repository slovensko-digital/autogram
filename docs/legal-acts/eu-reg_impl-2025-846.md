# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/846 — cross-border identity matching of natural persons

## Executive summary

This act lays down rules for **cross-border identity matching of natural persons** when public sector bodies act as relying parties for online cross-border services, whether reliance is placed on a European Digital Identity Wallet (wallet) or on a notified electronic identification scheme. Its addressees are **Member States, public sector bodies acting as relying parties, parties acting on their behalf, registers relied upon by relying parties and centralised identity-matching systems**. It is wallet-related only in that Article 2(3) and Recital 4 take the person identification data set of [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2977](eu-reg_impl-2024-2977.md) as the wallet input dataset.

Under the confirmed scope, Autogram is a **desktop document signing and verification application** with a localhost HTTP API and a CLI; it is not a wallet, not a notified eID scheme, not a public sector body, not a wallet-relying party and not a party performing identity matching against a register. The operative duties below are therefore scoped out, not reported as completed or breached. A local API, use of an identity card token for signing and validation of document signatures do not establish a relying-party or identity-matching role.

The act was adopted on **6 May 2025**, published on **7 May 2025** and entered into force on **27 May 2025** (twentieth day after publication). Its substantive rules **apply from 24 December 2026** ([Article 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/846/oj#art_6)); this is a legal application date for Member States/relying parties, not an Autogram release deadline. The act contains **no annex**. No amending act, corrigendum or consolidated version was identified in the retrieved official records. No national identity-matching scheme, register integration or wallet deployment was audited. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice or certification.
