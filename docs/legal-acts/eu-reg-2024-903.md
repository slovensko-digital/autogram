# Regulation (EU) 2024/903 — Interoperable Europe Act

## Executive summary

The Regulation principally addresses **Union entities and public sector bodies regulating, providing, managing or implementing trans-European digital public services**, not the independent publisher of Autogram's local desktop application, API or CLI. It does not establish a general software-vendor interoperability certification obligation or a JAdES implementation deadline. Public-service procurement, sharing and interoperability criteria can nevertheless affect Autogram **indirectly**. Participation in a regulatory sandbox, reuse and adaptation of a publicly shared solution, or voluntary Community membership can bring specific provisions into play; none is established on the shared facts.

The [register's shared facts](README.md#shared-facts) establish the assessment scope, not public-administration deployment, portal admission or sandbox participation. No public-service procurement contract, public-body mandate, sharing/adaptation agreement, Community registration or sandbox plan has been inspected. There is consequently no established unfulfilled statutory duty on this publisher and no `Not done` finding. Code inspection alone would not resolve these organisational questions.

Entry into force: **11 April 2024**; general application: **12 July 2024**; Article 3(1)–(4) and Article 17: **12 January 2025**. The assessment date is **8 October 2026**; these dates concern the specified addressees, not independent-vendor release deadlines.
