# Directive (EU) 2019/882 — European Accessibility Act

## Executive summary

The European Accessibility Act (EAA) regulates **enumerated consumer products and services**, not all software or all desktop applications. Autogram desktop, local API and CLI are not, merely by being signing software, consumer computer hardware, an operating system, an e-reader or another Article 2(1) product. No covered service operated by this publisher is established. Paid support and publication under its name do not by themselves establish EAA scope; the separate CRA manufacturer question must not be imported here.

Autogram could nevertheless be a component of a covered consumer banking or e-commerce service: Annex I IV(e)(i) and IV(g)(iii) expressly include electronic signatures. Such deployment, the responsible service provider, consumer contracts and accessibility performance are **Unknown**. These are not automatically independent-vendor duties. **eIDAS Article 15 is a separate conditional route** for end-user products used in providing eID/trust services, including where the publisher is not a QTSP; the deployment needed to resolve that route remains Unknown. EAA Article 15 below instead concerns presumptions of conformity.

Adopted **17 April 2019**, published **7 June 2019**, entered into force **27 June 2019**. National transposition was due **28 June 2022**; national measures apply from **28 June 2025**, subject to Article 32 transitions and the optional emergency-answering deferral to **28 June 2027**. There is no blanket 2030 grace period for new services. Slovak and other national transposition, enforcement and any broader national scope require separate assessment. No accessibility conformity, exemption or statutory breach is proved here.

### Evidence keys and reading conventions

Each key in the evidence column supplies the specific reason or missing evidence for that row, not a claim of compliance:

- **C** — the linked original PDF establishes interpretative/institutional context, not an Autogram software task; deliberately contextual (**Ignored**).
- **P** — Article 2(1), read with Article 3(38)–(42) and the confirmed desktop/API/CLI scope, does not enumerate standalone signing software. No covered product role is established; product-chain duties are scoped out, not knowingly disregarded.
- **O** — a different sector/actor (transport, media, communications, e-books, emergency services or buildings) is absent from confirmed scope.
- **D** — need an actual consumer-service deployment diagram, service-provider identity, consumer terms, integration/support contract and allocation of accessibility responsibilities. Conditional rows apply **only if the organisation actually provides a covered service or the identified covered-service deployment triggers the described input requirement**. A supplier does not automatically become the service provider.
- **T** — need task-based accessibility tests of the deployed UI/documentation: screen reader and accessibility tree, keyboard/focus, zoom/contrast/spacing, non-colour/non-audio alternatives, understandable error flows, timeouts and assistive-device interoperability as relevant to the row. No such conformance test report was obtained. Annex I product requirements are **Indirect/Unknown** as possible service-input criteria under III(a), not standalone EAA product duties for Autogram.
- **N** — need the relevant country's official transposition text, competent-authority designation, enforcement rules and any national options; this directive alone does not prove national implementation.
- **B** — need a dated, requirement-specific fundamental-alteration/burden assessment, cost/benefit and turnover records, accessibility funding terms, enterprise-size records and authority notices. No exemption is assumed.
- **H** — need the applicable OJ standards-reference/technical-specification notice, exact edition and scope mapping, plus an actual conformity report; standards were not inspected.

Relevance and status use the independent [README vocabulary](README.md#table-vocabulary). No row is marked Done or Not done: neither applicable fulfilment nor an applicable demonstrably unfulfilled measure is established. Numbered introductions and parent clauses appear as contextual rows where they set a rule; purely structural captions appear as headings. Subparagraphs in a numbered paragraph are explicitly identified when the source separates them. “Indent” labels identify printed unnumbered list items, not invented statutory numbering. Dates shown are act dates/triggers, not new vendor deadlines.
