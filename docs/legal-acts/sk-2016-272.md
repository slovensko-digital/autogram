# Slovakia — Act No 272/2016 Coll. on trust services

## Executive summary

**Assessment and source access: 8 October 2026. Repository baseline: `5ca91d5c`.** Scope is Autogram desktop, local API and CLI, not the browser extension or a hosted service. This is a reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit. **Every English description below is an unofficial paraphrase; the Slovak legal text controls.**

The organisation is not a qualified trust-service provider (QTSP) and operates no Autogram service receiving users' documents or personal data. Most QTSP duties therefore do not directly apply. This is **not a blanket exemption**: §§ 13–14 cover misuse of the EU trust mark and breach of conditions for using qualified services; § 8(4) addresses principals, representatives and employers, not just issuers; § 14(6) addresses non-qualified providers; and § 10 permits a wallet provider to contract with a QTSP instead of itself being qualified. These activities and organisational procedures remain unverified. Software supplied to a trust-service operator may also have indirect technical significance, without making the publisher that operator.

Inspected code uses EU LOTL, selected national trusted lists and online CRL/OCSP sources. This establishes an integration path, not complete Slovak validation, historical-signature or qualified-service conformity. The shared register records a successful SK TLv6/EU LOTL live check on **3 October 2026**, eight default countries and unsupported JAdES creation; none establishes EU-wide completeness or a statutory breach by this independent publisher. Public-authority acceptance/creation sanctions in § 14(3)–(4) are not independent-vendor deadlines.

The official static history lists **1 January 2025 onward**, incorporating Act 365/2024, as its latest consolidation. The complete HTML body and footnotes were obtained. **The linked legally binding consolidated PDF returned HTTP 404; the current dynamic portal yielded no readable content.** Accordingly, this document covers the retrieved official HTML reading text, not an independently authenticated PDF or guaranteed up-to-date dynamic-portal record.
