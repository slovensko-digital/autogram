# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1946 — qualified preservation of qualified signatures and seals

## Executive summary

This act specifies the adapted standards supporting the eIDAS presumption for **qualified preservation services for qualified electronic signatures and qualified electronic seals** (eIDAS Articles 34(2) and 40). Its operative obligations are addressed to **preservation service providers** — qualified trust service providers that preserve signatures/seals and their validation evidence over time. Autogram is a local desktop/CLI/API signing and validation application; it provides **no qualified preservation service**, and saving a signed file to the user's disk is **not** a qualified preservation service. The act is therefore **not directly applicable** to the publisher, and no obligation here imposes a vendor implementation deadline on Autogram.

The act's own standards route is **indirectly** relevant where it touches signature validation: its Annex adapts **ETSI TS 119 172-4** (signature applicability/validation policy), which is also the validation policy invoked by [Regulation (EU) 2025/1945](eu-reg_impl-2025-1945.md). Inspected source shows DSS-based validation and reporting, trusted-list loading, third-party timestamp requests and local file saving, but **no preservation service, no preservation-evidence packaging and no ENISA agreed-cryptographic-mechanisms policy**. Whether any validation performed by Autogram should track this act's adapted validation policy is **Unknown**; missing proof is not a demonstrated statutory violation. Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **5ca91d5c**. Adopted **29 September 2025**, published **30 September 2025**, effective **20 October 2025**.

## Provision-by-provision assessment

The act's substantive duties bind **preservation service providers (PSPs) / qualified trust service providers**. Because the publisher is not a QTSP, operates no preservation service and stores signed files only locally, those duties are **None/Ignored**; only the validation-policy adaptation in Annex point 2 has an indirect interoperability bearing on Autogram's validation function. No row is **Direct**. Dates marked “Effective” refer to 20 October 2025, not a vendor implementation deadline.
