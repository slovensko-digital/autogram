# Directive (EU) 2024/2853 — revised Product Liability Directive

## Executive summary

**Conditional relevance; elevated manufacturer/liability risk, not an established compliance failure.** Software is expressly a product. The organisation publishes Autogram under its own name and charges above cost recovery for support ([shared facts](README.md#shared-facts)). Those facts require a serious examination of commercial supply and own-name manufacturer status, but the support contracts, distribution arrangements and relationship between support and software supply have not been reviewed. The exclusion for free and open-source software developed or supplied outside commercial activity cannot simply be assumed. Conversely, paid support does not, without the missing facts, settle every supply's classification. **PLD classification must be assessed under this Directive, not inferred from the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).**

This is a **no-fault civil liability and compensation framework**, implemented through Member State law, not a blanket certification, CE-marking, signature-format implementation or product approval duty. A defect, covered damage and causation matter; security requirements can inform defectiveness and evidential presumptions. Neither a software bug nor a missing certificate automatically establishes liability. Recital 51 expressly says this Directive does not itself impose an obligation to provide updates or upgrades, although controlled software and missing safety updates can defeat a liability defence.

The act entered into force on **8 December 2024**. Member States must transpose it by **9 December 2026**. **Article 2(1), corrected on 7 May 2026, covers products placed on the market or put into service after 8 December 2026 — from 9 December 2026 inclusive.** The original wording “after 9 December 2026” is superseded. Directive 85/374/EEC continues for products placed on the market or put into service before 9 December 2026. Substantial modifications need separate treatment; ordinary updates are not automatically new products or a restarted expiry period. Slovak transposition, national procedural rules, release/supply dates and any actual loss/claim remain unverified.

Scope: desktop application, local API and CLI; no browser-extension or hosted-service assessment. Assessment date **8 October 2026**, baseline **5ca91d5c**. This is a reading aid, **not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit**.

## Provision-by-provision assessment

Vocabulary follows [README.md](README.md#table-vocabulary). **Conditional / Unknown** means the role, triggering event or fulfilment is not established, not a finding of wrongdoing. **Indirect / Ignored** identifies interpretation, definitions or other actors' procedures, not a disregarded publisher obligation. All summaries are English paraphrases, not reproduced legal text.

Evidence labels used in the tables:

- **Supply dossier**: missing software licence/distribution terms, support contracts and invoices, price/cost allocation, recipients and territories, monetisation/data-use arrangements, publisher/manufacturer identity, release and first-supply dates. Shared facts establish own-name publication and above-cost support only.
- **Safety dossier**: missing release-specific risk assessments, security/validation test reports, installation/use warnings and claims, expected lifetime, dependency/component inventory, integration authorisations, update-control and delivery records, modification assessments and recall/intervention records. This is evidence needed to assess liability, not a PLD-created blanket documentation/certification duty.
- **Claim dossier**: missing claimant/defendant identity, medical/property/data-loss evidence, private/professional use, defect investigation, causation analysis, discovery dates and proceedings history. No particular injury, defect, court order or claim is established here.
- **National law**: missing verified Slovak implementing act and effective dates, any other relevant Member State implementation, procedural/redress rules, development-risk derogations and notifications. EU dates are established independently of that gap.
- **Contract dossier**: missing component supplier/integrator contracts, warranties, recourse waivers, SME/linked-enterprise evidence and indemnity/insurance terms. Contractual allocation does not automatically bind an injured person.
