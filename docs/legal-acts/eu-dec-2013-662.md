# Commission Implementing Decision 2013/662/EU — historical trusted-list amendments

## Executive summary

This **Commission Implementing Decision**, adopted **14 October 2013** and applicable **1 February 2014**, amended Article 2 and replaced the Annex of Decision 2009/767/EC. Its establishment, publication, signing, notification and continuity duties address **Member States**, not Autogram's independent publisher. The Annex's relying-party interpretation rules are indirectly relevant to validating historical signatures: a listed CA alone does not prove that every certificate is qualified or that a signature used an SSCD.

Autogram delegates trusted-list ingestion to DSS and selects countries; this is evidence of an integration, not proof of compatibility with every historical list, qualification filter, status transition or certificate profile. Those outcomes remain **Unknown**. The shared 3 October 2026 SK TLv6/EU LOTL smoke-test result is not historical or EU-wide coverage. No applicable, demonstrably unfulfilled publisher duty is established here.

The 2013 template is **historical**, superseded for the eIDAS trusted-list regime by Decision (EU) 2015/1505. Do not confuse the UK archive's domestic **revocation** label with an EU repeal. No EU act expressly repealing 2013/662/EU was verified in this review. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit.

## Provision-by-provision assessment

### Reading key and evidence

All summaries concern the **obtained original OJ text**. `GR` means Annex General Requirements; `I`, `II`, `III` mean Annex chapters. Indents are numbered here **in source order** solely as locators; these are not invented official numbering. Unnumbered paragraphs are counted within each named heading, excluding introductory list stems where the stem only supplies the condition for the individually assessed children. Structural amendment wrappers and list stems are headings, not duplicated duties.

Evidence shorthand used in the tables:

- **H**: OJ PDF and government adopted HTML above. Historical Member-State/scheme-operator requirement, not a duty on this publisher; status **Ignored** deliberately scopes out that actor's compliance. It does not assert Member-State compliance.
- **C**: same sources; interpretative, definitional, optional or commencement context, not an implementation task; **Ignored** for that reason.
- **T**: historical relying-party compatibility remains **Unknown**. Inspected [SignatureValidator.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java), `initialize`, lines **91–132**: DSS TL job, LOTL authentication source, pivot support, selected-country predicate, caches, trusted-certificate source and CRL/OCSP sources; `getJournalCertificateSource`, lines **135–142**, loads the bundled trust keystore. These establish delegation/configuration only. Needed: archived TS 119 612 v1.1.1 list fixtures, certificates, status histories, expected qualification/SSCD/legal-person outcomes, path-building and revocation cases, and tests against the exact shipped DSS version. No such complete matrix was inspected.
- `p.` below is the **OJ page**, not the PDF page. All operative amendments apply from **1 February 2014**; annex rows describe the historical template, not new 2026 app deadlines.

Direct EUR-Lex retrieval is an access limitation, not a reason to invent missing provisions: the archived official publication supplied the text.
- **Enumeration checked:** sequential PDF/HTML comparison across GR headings **1, 2, 2.1 categories 1–6, 3.1–3.5, 4**, both figures, **17 defined terms** (CA's two numbered alternatives separately), Chapter I's opening rule and five field headings, Chapter II's **7 operative indents**, Chapter III's opening paragraph and content indents including **4 field-display subpoints and 16 certificate-display items**. Repeated qualification rules under GR 3.4 and Chapter I are assessed at both occurrences. Structural headings/list stems do not create duplicate parent duties. Figure components and legend are identified; the published diagrams remain the full source for precise arrow paths, not a newly reproduced transition matrix.
- **No unavailable annex text or omitted operative annex points identified.** The signature/date block identifies adoption and Commissioner Michel Barnier, not additional duties. Footnotes (19 in archive HTML plus definition-table note) are separately identified; page-local OJ numbering differs. The legal text is linked, not reproduced.
- **Standards not inspected:** TS 119 612 v1.1.1 (especially incorporated clauses 5–6), EN 319 411-2, EN 319 412-5, TS 103 172, RFC 2119 and referenced ISO standards. Their complete requirements are separate works and **not covered** by these act-text tables. National scheme documents and live/historical scheme URIs were not inspected.
- **Version/status limits:** no later EU corrigendum/amendment/repeal of this amending act was obtained. Historical supersession is supported by the later 2015/1505 template and 1 July 2016 migration; express EU repeal is **unverified** and UK revocation must not be substituted for it. Archived metadata is not current 2026 EU status evidence. Base-act consolidation and later amendments to 2015/1505 require their own reviews.
- **Engineering evidence outstanding:** the T matrix described above; historical list-signature algorithms and signing-key changes, integrity/tampering, empty lists, current/history status selection, root/subordinate mapping, mixed QC issuance, all qualification filters/qualifiers and missing-information defaults, independent CRL/OCSP trust, missing endpoint recovery, national non-QC scheme distinctions, selected/unselected-country effects and shipped DSS dependency behaviour. - **Actor limits:** no Member-State list-publication, scheme-operation, supervisory, accreditation or public-sector recognition role is established for this publisher.
