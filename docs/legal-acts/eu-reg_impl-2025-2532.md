# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2532 — reference standards and specifications for qualified electronic archiving services

## Executive summary

This act designates, under eIDAS Article 45j(2) as introduced by [Regulation (EU) 2024/1183](eu-reg-2024-1183.md), the reference standard and adaptations for **qualified electronic archiving services**: **CEN/TS 18170:2025**, applied with nine sets of adaptations (Annex points **(a)**–**(i)**) covering normative references, policy/practice statements, terms and conditions, human resources, cryptographic controls, network requirements, evidence collection, termination and reliable time.

**Who it addresses.** Every operative provision addresses **providers of qualified electronic archiving services** and their archiving trust service provider (termed **EATSP**), together with supervisory bodies and the Commission. It is not, on its face, an obligation on an independent signing or validation application.

**Relevance to Autogram: predominantly None (context).** The inspected repository implements a client-side desktop/CLI application and a locally hosted HTTP API that **create and validate** signatures/seals and **save the resulting file to a local path**. There is no receipt/storage/retrieval/deletion lifecycle, no preservation period, no retention schedule, no OAIS/AIP model and no archiving service. **Local file saving is not a qualified electronic archiving service.** The act therefore establishes no **Direct** obligation on this publisher, and no **Not done** status: missing evidence of a service capability is not a demonstrated breach of an inapplicable duty.

**What remains relevant.** Two narrow touchpoints are recorded rather than dismissed:

- **Indirect (input/interoperability context).** A QEA service consumes qualified signatures, qualified seals and qualified timestamps. Autogram can produce advanced/qualified signature and seal artifacts and can attach a timestamp through a configurable time-stamp authority, so the Annex's reliance on qualified trust services is interoperability context for those outputs. None of this makes Autogram an archiving service.
- **Conditional (unresolved role).** Whether Autogram is used in providing an eID/trust service is **Unknown**, and qualified electronic archiving is a trust service. If the organisation were to operate or supply a QEA service claiming the Article 45j(2) presumption, the Article 1 duty and the Annex adaptations would become applicable and would require reassessment. That condition is not currently established.

The only **Unknown** status attached to a directly technical claim concerns qualified timestamps: Autogram configures a time-stamp authority for `BASELINE_T` signatures but implements **no check that the chosen TSA is qualified**, and its default list merely contains endpoints whose URLs are named "qualified".

Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline **`5ca91d5c`**. Adopted **16 December 2025**, published **17 December 2025**, effective **6 January 2026**.

## Evidence key

All evidence below was inspected on 8 October 2026. Source inspection is not a released-binary audit and no builds or tests were run.

- **S — signature/seal creation:** [SigningJob.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java), `signWithKeyAndRespond`, lines 86–109, builds XAdES/CAdES/PAdES signatures (and ASiC containers) through DSS; `createSignatureService`, lines 130–149, selects the service and sets the timestamp source for `BASELINE_T`. Signing is not archiving.
- **F — local file save:** [SaveFileResponder.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/SaveFileResponder.java), lines 28–36, writes the signed artifact with `signedDocument.getDocument().save(targetFile.toString())`; [SaveFileFromBatchResponder.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/SaveFileFromBatchResponder.java), lines 28–37, does the same for batches; [TargetPath.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/TargetPath.java), `getSaveFilePath`, lines 147–182, resolves the local target path; [CliUI.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/cli/CliUI.java), `onDocumentSaved`, lines 179–183, only reports the saved file. Saving a file to disk is not receipt, storage, retrieval and deletion over a preservation period.
- **T — timestamps:** [SigningJob.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java), lines 146–147, attaches the configured `TSPSource` only for `BASELINE_T`; [UserSettings.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/UserSettings.java), `setTsaServer`, lines 357–375, builds `OnlineTSPSource`s from a configurable list, and line 50 defines `DEFAULT_TSA_SERVER = "http://timestamp.sectigo.com/qualified,http://tsa.belgium.be/connect"`; [AppStarter.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/AppStarter.java), line 28, exposes the `--tsa-server` option; [DssSigningParametersFactory.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/DssSigningParametersFactory.java), lines 82–84, reserves space in PAdES `BASELINE_T`. **No qualification check of the TSA** is implemented; the URL text "qualified" is not verification.
- **V — validation:** [SignatureValidator.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SignatureValidator.java), `validate`, lines 76–81, delegates to DSS `validateDocument()` without an explicit policy argument; `initialize`, lines 91–133, configures LOTL/country filtering, trusted certificates and online CRL/OCSP sources. [pom.xml](../../pom.xml), lines 18 and 43–46, selects DSS **6.5**.
- **N — no archiving capability (negative evidence):** a case-insensitive search of `src/main/java` found **no** occurrence of "archiv", "preservation", "OAIS", "AIP" or "retention". The only archiving-adjacent strings are time-stamp authority URLs in [AppStarter.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/AppStarter.java), lines 83–84. There is no lifecycle, retention or preservation module to assess against CEN/TS 18170.

## Provision-by-provision assessment

`None / Ignored` below marks duties that address a **qualified electronic archiving service provider (EATSP)** or its supervisory relationships: Autogram is a client-side signing/validation application and its local file save is not an archiving service (F, N). `Indirect` marks interoperability/input criteria that touch artifacts Autogram can produce (qualified signatures/seals, timestamps). `Conditional / Unknown` marks provisions whose applicability turns on the unresolved question whether the publisher operates or supplies a QEA service claiming the Article 45j(2) presumption. Parent headings are structural; their individually identifiable children carry the assessment.

## Coverage and limitations

- Enumerated independently from the official act: **10 recitals; 3 articles (Article 1 has 2 numbered paragraphs, Article 2 and Article 3 each have 1 unnumbered paragraph); 1 binding closing paragraph; 1 adoption/signature formula; 3 preamble units; and the complete annex, whose opening paragraph is followed by points (a)–(i)**. These produce **58 assessment rows**: 3 preamble/recital-openers, 10 recital rows, 6 article/concluding rows and **39 annex rows**.
- Annex enumeration, individually identifiable: opening paragraph (1 row); **(a)** all 8 normative-reference entries; **(b)** 5 rows (CEN/TS 18170 clause 6.1; ETSI EN 319 401 clause 5; notification procedures; one-month notice; three-month notice); **(c)** 2 rows; **(d)** 2 rows; **(e)** 12 rows, in which the three device-certification options (a)/(b)/(c) are separated from the key-holding requirement and the remaining controls; **(f)** 4 rows; **(g)** 1 row; **(h)** 2 rows; **(i)** 2 rows. The point headings (a)–(i) are structural and are not duplicated as extra duties; each dash sub-item is given its own row.
- Official body text, all 10 recitals, all three articles, the closing formula, and the annex with both source footnotes were obtained in the English OJ XHTML described above; **no act provision was deliberately omitted**. No downloadable complete official PDF was obtained, and the direct EUR-Lex OJ page presented a WAF challenge, so the anchor identifiers were verified against the Cellar XHTML rather than the live ELI page. Documents marked complete here must not be read as independent PDF verification.
- **None of the external standards was inspected.** CEN/TS 18170:2025, ETSI EN 319 401, ETSI EN 319 421, ISO 14721, ISO/IEC 15408, FIPS PUB 140-3, the ENISA ACM-ECCG catalogue and the EUCC instruments CIR (EU) 2024/482 / 2024/3144 are separate works. Their cited titles/editions and the act's adaptations were inspected as printed; their clauses are not imported or reproduced.
- **Decisive scope finding.** Autogram creates and validates signatures/seals (S, V) and saves the result to a local path (F); it has no archiving, preservation, retention or OAIS/AIP capability (N). **Local file saving is not a qualified electronic archiving service**, so the operative QEA-provider/EATSP duties are recorded as None/Ignored rather than Not done. That classification reflects an inapplicable actor, not established compliance.
- **Residual unknowns.** Whether the publisher operates or supplies a QEA service claiming the Article 45j(2) presumption; whether a consuming QEA service relies on Autogram-produced signatures/seals or timestamps; and whether any configured TSA is qualified. These underpin the Conditional/Unknown and Indirect/Unknown rows and are the only items requiring further evidence if the scope changes.
- No builds, test execution, output generation, commits or organisational compliance claims accompany this reading register. Inspected named symbols establish narrow technical facts (creation, validation, local saving, timestamp configuration), not conformity to CEN/TS 18170 or its adaptations, and not a released-binary audit.
