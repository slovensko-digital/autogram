# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/1506 — historical public-sector signature and seal formats

## Executive summary

This Decision specified the advanced-signature and advanced-seal formats that **Member States must recognise for public-sector online services**, together with an alternative-format validation route. It did not require an independent application publisher to implement every format or operate a free online validator. Its relevance to Autogram is **Indirect**: interoperability and historical acceptance criteria, not a direct publisher duty.

The Decision entered into force on **29 September 2015** and was **repealed from 23 February 2026** by [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/248](eu-reg_impl-2026-248.md), Article 5. Its last day of validity was **22 February 2026**. The successor's Articles 1(2) and 3(2) require recognition of its Annex II legacy formats for signatures/seals **created before 23 February 2028**. That is a creation-date cutoff, not a sunset for recognition of already-created qualifying documents, and it does not keep the repealed Decision in force. Current recognition rests on the successor Regulation.

Autogram's inspected creation path selects XAdES, CAdES, PAdES and associated XAdES/CAdES containers; this proves specific service selection, **not** complete conformity with the old version-specific ETSI profiles, LT creation/validation, qualified status, seal handling or EU-wide public-sector acceptance. JAdES is unsupported on that path, but was not listed in this Decision; successor-format analysis belongs in the linked document. No statutory publisher violation is established by these gaps. This is a reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit.

### Evidence and reading conventions

- **E1:** [SigningJob.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/core/SigningJob.java), `createSignatureService`, lines **130–149**, inspected both in the working tree and using `git show 5ca91d5c:…`. XAdES/CAdES use standalone or ASiC services; PAdES uses its PDF service; the default branch rejects unsupported forms, including JAdES. Timestamp-source selection is conditional. Service names are not a profile-conformance certificate.
- **E2:** [ServerSigningParameters.java](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/server/dto/ServerSigningParameters.java), `validate`, lines **324–345**, inspected in the working tree: explicitly allowed legacy-API levels are XAdES/PAdES/CAdES BASELINE_B and BASELINE_T; PAdES rejects a container. This is evidence of that path only, not a claim that every application path or validator is limited to those levels. This file's baseline identity was not independently checked.
- **H:** Articles 1–4 below describe the **historical** recognition/validation regime. They are not revived by the successor's legacy-format rule. Rows marked Ignored deliberately scope out an inapplicable historical public-sector duty; Unknown technical rows mean conformity evidence is missing, not that the publisher has breached a statute.
- **V:** For alternative-format validation, the missing evidence is the offering Member State's designated validation facility/specification, representative cross-border results, and fixtures for the particular condition. The organisation is not established to be that Member State, its delegated public-sector service operator, or the designated validator. A future public-sector deployment would require its contract, delegation and acceptance policy to assess responsibilities; none is available here.

## Coverage and limitations

- **Complete adopted English text obtained:** all five original OJ pages (37–41) via the UK official archive PDF and the as-adopted XML. Compared the PDF's recital numbering, article/paragraph/letter/number structure and annex table entries against the XML. Direct EUR-Lex HTML/PDF retrieval returned empty HTTP 202 responses; that access limitation was bypassed for the adopted text using the official archive, not filled with secondary paraphrases.
- **Enumerated coverage:** all **10 recitals**; Articles **1–5**; all **four numbered paragraphs** in Articles 2 and 4; all **six lettered subpoints** and **16 subordinate numbered conditions**; the unnumbered paragraphs in Articles 1, 3 and 5; the closing binding/direct-applicability paragraph; both annex sections, all **four prose requirement paragraphs** and **eight specification table entries**. Chapeaux are explicitly identified. No recital, operative paragraph or annex requirement is omitted. Title, legal-basis citations, adoption/signatory block and citation/link footnotes were read; they contain no additional assessed duty, and their dates/references are recorded above.
- **Successor verification is limited to indexed official EUR-Lex text/metadata**, not a successfully downloaded complete 2026/248 act: repeal, last-validity date, reference substitution, creation-date cutoff and the Annex II legacy relationship were checked there. The full successor annex comparison and all its individual provisions belong to the cross-linked successor assessment. The cross-link target was not present when this document was drafted; this task does not create or edit it.
- **External ETSI standards were not inspected.** All four exact identifiers/versions and annex links were read, but their technical clauses are separate works. No claim of complete ETSI conformity, modern-standard equivalence or automatic qualification follows from using DSS services.
- Outstanding evidence: a version-specific signature/seal/container conformance corpus including B/T/LT and historical validation; public-sector acceptance results; pseudonym/QSCD/qualified-certificate adverse-case output checks; any state validation designation, facility specification and service/delegation contract. Neither the shared 3 October 2026 SK TLv6/EU LOTL live success nor code service selection establishes EU-wide or historical validation correctness. No builds, tests or released-binary audit were performed for this document.
