# Directive (EU) 2022/2555 — NIS2

## Executive summary

**Assessment: 8 October 2026; repository baseline: `5ca91d5c`.** NIS2 regulates covered entities and national cybersecurity institutions, not every software product. Publishing Autogram as a local desktop application, local API and CLI does **not inherently establish coverage**, nor does using trust services make the publisher a trust-service provider. The [shared facts](README.md#shared-facts) establish that the organisation is not a qualified trust-service provider and operates no Autogram service receiving users’ documents or personal data. They do not establish that the organisation has no other covered activity.

Whether the organisation separately qualifies as an essential or important entity is **Unknown**. In particular, paid support must be assessed against the managed-service-provider definition and actual contracts: neither payment nor the label “support” alone decides coverage. Sector, size (including linked/partner enterprises), jurisdiction, national identification and any critical-entity designation remain unverified. Covered customers may also impose supplier security terms without making the publisher a covered entity.

The principal conditional organisational measures concern management accountability/training, risk management, supplier security and incident reporting (Articles 20–23). No applicable unmet measure is established here; **Not done** is therefore not assigned. Source code is not evidence of organisational compliance. The Directive’s **17 October 2024 transposition deadline** and **18 October 2024 application of national measures** concern Member States; they are not automatic independent-software-vendor deadlines. National transposition, implementing regulations and actual entity obligations require separate assessments.

## Coverage and limitations

- Obtained and inspected the **complete original English official PDF**, OJ pp. 80–152: **144 recitals, Articles 1–46 and Annexes I–III**. Tables separately enumerate numbered paragraphs (including all **41 Article 6 definitions**), lettered and roman subpoints, operative unnumbered subparagraphs, both deletion blocks, all Annex I/II listed entity types and all **75 Annex III correlation rows**. Multi-paragraph recitals 30 and 33 and recital 91’s five criteria are separately identifiable. Introductory treaty/procedure citations, signature attestation, chapter/sector headings and bibliographic footnotes are source context, not additional requirements; their referenced works are not imported into these tables.
- Coverage was checked against PDF-to-text extraction in published order, checking each article’s paragraph/letter sequence, additional subparagraphs after lists, annex sectors/subsectors/indents and the complete final correlation table. PDF page furniture and line wraps are not provisions. The original Annex III literally refers to **Article 11(2)(a)–(d)** and **11(2)(f)** although the operational task lettering is in Article 11(3); these printed mappings are preserved, not silently repaired. A current corrigendum check remains necessary.
- No part of the obtained original operative text or annexes is intentionally omitted. **Current consolidation/amendments/corrigenda remain unverified**, so this is not a currency certification as of the assessment date. In particular, later implementing/delegated legislation and guidance under Articles 21/23/24 are separate texts and are not assessed by this document. The deletion of eIDAS Article 19 must also be read with the separately amended eIDAS framework; it is not repeal of all trust-provider security duties.
- **National transposition is separate.** This document does not establish Slovak or another Member State’s current scope, registration dates, competent authority, applicable exemptions, sanctions or implementation status. The Directive’s national application date must not be substituted for an entity-specific vendor obligation without examining that law and the organisation’s actual role.
- No ETSI standard was inspected or imported. Referenced ISO/IEC standards (including cloud terminology, vulnerability handling/disclosure and security-management series), NACE classifications, the SME Recommendation, referenced sectoral instruments and external guidance were not independently audited as complete works. Their references are not proof of full technical conformity.
- No source-code lines are cited because no organisational measure is established by code in this assessment. Shared local/non-QTSP facts are taken from the register baseline, not independently re-audited. Outstanding evidence is specifically **E1–E5** above: especially support contracts/actual active administration, all other organisational activities, size/ownership data, national scope/designation, board/training/risk-control records, supplier terms, incident logs and registration/supervisory records.
- **No Done/Not done compliance verdict is supported.** Conditional organisational obligations remain **Unknown**; institutional/contextual units are **Ignored** with reasons. This deliberately distinguishes lack of evidence from a statutory breach and indirect supplier expectations from direct NIS2 coverage.
