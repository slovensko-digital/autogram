# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/296 — historical eID-scheme cooperation

## Executive summary

This Decision organised **Member-State cooperation on notified or intended-to-be-notified electronic identification schemes**, information exchange, peer review and the Cooperation Network. It did not impose a peer-review, notification or certification obligation on a local electronic-signature application merely because that application uses an eID card to sign. Autogram's desktop application, local API and CLI, and its publisher, have no established Member-State, scheme-notifier or Cooperation Network role in the confirmed scope.

The Decision entered into force on **17 March 2015** and was **repealed on 19 August 2025** by Article 7 of [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1568](eu-reg_impl-2025-1568.md). Retained here for historical traceability, not as a current software checklist. Every assessed unit is **Ignored**, with an individual reason below; that means scoped out, not disregarding an applicable obligation. Participation as an invited expert or observer could historically have attracted confidentiality duties, but no such appointment is established. Other eIDAS provisions, including the unresolved end-user-product question under Article 15, require separate analysis: this Decision does not settle them.

Assessment date: **8 October 2026**; repository baseline: **5ca91d5c**. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit.
