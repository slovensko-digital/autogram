# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1570 — certified signature/seal device notifications

## Executive summary

This act specifies **Member State notifications to the Commission**, not a software-vendor filing obligation. It covers certified qualified electronic signature creation devices (QSCDs) and qualified electronic seal creation devices, including changes and cancellation of certification. Under the register's established facts, Autogram's publisher is neither the notifying State nor a qualified trust-service provider.

Device identity, configuration, certification status and validity dates are **indirect local signing/validation inputs**. Connecting to a token or displaying a qualified-signature result does not establish that the particular hardware/software configuration has valid certification. Actual device certification and configuration matching remain **Unknown**; no applicable unfulfilled vendor notification duty is established.

Adopted **29 July 2025**, published **30 July 2025**, in force **19 August 2025**, applicable **19 November 2025**. The original text's 19 December application date was corrected on **20 August 2025**. Assessment/source access: **8 October 2026**. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice or certification.

### Evidence boundaries

**E1 — local device connection:** [`PKCS11TokenDriver.createToken`, lines 11–17](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/drivers/PKCS11TokenDriver.java#L11-L17) constructs a native PKCS#11 token connection using a configured driver path and slot. This establishes an integration path, not certification, exact certified component versions or State notification.

**E2 — qualification display:** [`SignatureBadgeFactory.createBadgeFromQualification`, lines 68–94](../../src/main/java/digital/slovensko/autogram/ui/gui/SignatureBadgeFactory.java#L68-L94) maps DSS qualification values, including QESIG, QESEAL and unknown QSCD-related values, to badges. This does not prove an authoritative device-register lookup or certification validity for every signature.

**Missing device dossier:** exact device/product identifiers, hardware/firmware/software versions, certificate of conformity, certification reports/security targets, effective/expiry dates, current notified status and tests matching the actual deployed configuration to its certification. Organisational notification evidence would instead be a State submission receipt, update record and accompanying documents; no unrelated application code can establish it.

## Coverage and limitations

- **Complete official Spanish original and Spanish corrigendum were obtained**, not merely a secondary synopsis. Enumeration was checked directly against the BOE full texts: five recitals; Article 1's two numbered paragraphs; Article 2's two unnumbered paragraphs; final binding/direct-applicability formula; annex points 1–14, including 1(a)–(d), 3(a)–(b) and both dashes, and 14(a)–(e); the sole corrigendum replacement block. Parent points 1, 3 and 14 are identified structurally rather than duplicated as duties. Legal basis is also assessed. Signature/date formula and four bibliographic footnotes contain no additional operative requirements and are not separate task rows.
- **English complete-source access remains a limitation:** direct EUR-Lex English HTML/PDF requests returned no readable text. Official English search extracts corroborated operative provisions/annex but are not a complete-text retrieval. Recital summaries and complete structural coverage rely on the retrieved official Spanish text. No portion of that retrieved original/annex was omitted from the structural assessment. A complete authoritative English retrieval and multilingual corrigendum/version-history check remain outstanding.
- **No standard entries appear in this act's annex:** it specifies notification data, not a standards list. No ETSI or other external technical standard was inspected or imported; no certification reports, security targets or alternative certification procedures were inspected. The referenced eIDAS provisions and data-protection instruments are separate works, not additional text of this act.
- Source inspection is narrowly limited to E1/E2. It proves token integration and qualification-display mapping, not device certification, Commission-register consumption, State fulfilment, all qualified-signature outcomes or organisational compliance. No build, test, implementation or binary audit was performed.
- Outstanding evidence: the device dossier identified above, authoritative current/historical device-status records, tests for configuration/time/status matching, and (only if evaluating the responsible State) notification receipts and updates. Missing evidence is not proof of a statutory violation; application support does not transfer State notification duties to the vendor.
