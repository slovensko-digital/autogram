# Slovakia — Act No 305/2013 Coll. on the electronic form of exercising public authority (e-Government Act)

## Executive summary

**Assessment and source access: 8 October 2026. Repository baseline: `5ca91d5c`.** Scope is Autogram desktop, local API and CLI, not the browser extension or a hosted service. This is a reading aid, not legal advice, certification or a released-binary audit. **Every English description below is an unofficial paraphrase; the Slovak legal text controls.**

Act 305/2013 Coll. (the *e-Government Act*) governs how Slovak public authorities exercise public authority electronically: access points, shared modules, electronic mailboxes, identification/authentication, **authorisation**, **electronic submissions and electronic official documents**, electronic delivery, guaranteed conversion, payments and reference registers. The subject of this reading — electronic submissions, authorisation and document formats — is concentrated in Part Three (§§ 17–34) and Part Four (§§ 35–39).

Most of the Act addresses **public authorities, the central portal and shared-module administrators**, not an independent software publisher. The organisation is not a public authority, does not operate the central portal, shared modules, the electronic mailbox module, the central electronic registry (podateľňa) or guaranteed conversion, and does not file submissions on anyone's behalf. Those duties are therefore **None / Ignored** here, and the filer/authority duties in §§ 23–28 are not transferred to Autogram merely because it can produce documents in the Act's formats. A smaller set of provisions is **interoperability context**: the Act's definitions of electronic form, electronic submission and electronic official document, and the format/authorisation/timestamp rules, describe inputs and outputs that a signing tool may create or consume.

Repo evidence shows an **e-form and XMLDataContainer (XDC) interoperability path**, not statutory compliance. Autogram builds `xdc:XMLDataContainer` / `xdc:XMLData` documents, references or embeds XSD/XSLT schemas and binds their digests (`XDCBuilder.java`); it recognises `http://data.gov.sk/def/container/xmldatacontainer+xml/1.1` (`EFormUtils.java`); it auto-loads e-form resources from the ÚPVS static dataset at `slovensko.sk` and other named e-form sources (`UpvsEFormResources.java`, `EFormResourcesBuilder.java`); and it declares the `application/vnd.gov.sk.xmldatacontainer+xml` MIME type (`AutogramMimeType.java`). It also signs with qualified certificates and can attach a **qualified timestamp** through a configured TSA source when the BASELINE_T profile is selected (`SigningJob.java`, `UserSettings.java`, `DssSigningParametersFactory.java`). None of this establishes that an authority's electronic submission or official document satisfies §§ 23–28, nor that Autogram itself performs identification, authentication, authorisation, delivery or guaranteed conversion.

**Major unknowns:** whether the organisation ever acts as a filer/participant under the Act or supplies software into a public-authority e-Government workflow; whether Autogram output matches the exact formats required by the implementing decrees (notably the uniform message format and e-form/XDC schemas); and whether any qualified timestamp obtained via a public TSA satisfies the Act's "qualified electronic timestamp" conditions in a given case. The Act's complete text was retrieved as official static HTML; the linked **legally binding consolidated PDF returned HTTP 404**, and the dynamic portal record is a JavaScript shell that served no readable body (same blocker recorded for [Act 272/2016](sk-2016-272.md)).
