# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1731 — wallet standards update

**Register:** [Autogram legal-act register](README.md) · **Assessment date:** 8 October 2026 · **Repository baseline:** `5ca91d5c`. **Amends:** [2024/2977](eu-reg_impl-2024-2977.md), [2024/2979](eu-reg_impl-2024-2979.md), [2024/2980](eu-reg_impl-2024-2980.md), [2024/2982](eu-reg_impl-2024-2982.md).

## Executive summary

**What this act is.** An amending regulation updating the **reference standards and specifications** of the EUDI-Wallet implementing regulations, notably a protection regime for the wallet user's **portrait** and timing for gatekeeper interoperability. Adopted **15 July 2026**, published **22 July 2026**.

**Relevance to Autogram: None / Indirect (context).** Autogram is not a wallet, wallet provider, wallet-relying party or gatekeeper, so the amendments create no duty on it. The only indirect interest is standards-convergence if Autogram were ever embedded in a wallet flow. This document is a register entry that records the amendment operations and cross-links the amended acts; the amended acts themselves carry their own documents.

**Dates.** Adopted 15 Jul 2026; publication 22 Jul 2026; entry into force per Article 5; some deferred dates in Article 4 (e.g. Article 3(4) from 11 August 2028) concern the amended regulations.

## Coverage and limitations

**Text obtained.** Official English XHTML; **all 9 recitals** and **Articles 1–5** with their numbered amendment points were enumerated. The act amends four wallet implementing regulations; their consolidated texts and annexes are **not re-assessed here** — each has (or will have) its own register document, and the amending act does not create independent obligations for a signing application. The many `ANNEX` markers in the source are quoted annexes of the amended regulations, not new annexes of this act.

**Status rationale.** Every row is **None / Ignored** because Autogram is not a wallet actor; recital (7) is **Indirect/Unknown** only as a pointer to the [GDPR](eu-reg-2016-679.md) assessment. If Autogram later integrates a wallet, this act becomes context for the standards the wallet applies, not a duty on Autogram.
