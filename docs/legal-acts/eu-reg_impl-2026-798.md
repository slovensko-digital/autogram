# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/798 — remote onboarding of users to the European Digital Identity Wallets

## Executive summary

This act sets out, for Article 5a(24) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183), the **reference standards and specifications for the remote onboarding of users to the European Digital Identity Wallets** by electronic identification means at assurance level substantial combined with additional remote onboarding procedures meeting assurance level high. It is implemented essentially by one external standard, **ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1 (2025-02)**, whose listed clauses apply subject to 16 specific adaptations in the Annex.

The act was verified to exist in the official Publications Office/Cellar record, adopted **7 April 2026**, published in OJ L on **8 April 2026** (L series 2026/798), CELEX **32026R0798**. Article 2 makes entry into force the twentieth day after publication, i.e. **28 April 2026**. It does **not** amend or replace another act; it lays down rules for the application of eIDAS and is a new instrument. No consolidation or amending act was found as of the access date.

This act addresses **Member States, providers of person identification data (PID) and identity proofing service providers (IPSPs)** performing wallet onboarding. Under the confirmed scope, Autogram is a desktop application that signs and validates **documents**; it is not a wallet provider, PID provider, IPSP or wallet-relying party, and the repository contains no wallet, onboarding, identity-proofing, face-image or biometric code. The requirements are therefore scoped out (**None/Ignored**), not reported as completed or breached. The referenced ETSI/EN standards are separate works and were not inspected. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice or certification.

## Coverage and limitations

No portion was unavailable or deliberately omitted; the footnote bibliographic citations and the signature/publication block were read as sources, not counted as separate obligations.
- **Enumerated units assessed:** **10 recitals** (`rct_1`–`rct_10`); **3 article paragraphs** (Art. 1 paragraph 1; Art. 2 entry-into-force and binding/applicability paragraphs); and the Annex = **1 preamble paragraph**, **9 listed applicable clauses** in section 1, and **16 numbered adaptation points** in section 2 expanded into **26 distinct units** (20 requirement codes, 6 notes/examples, plus 3 entries marked `void`). **Total assessment rows: 49.** The Annex's structural title and section headings are labels, not extra duties; each numbered adaptation point is identifiable through the requirement rows labelled `§2(n)`.
- **Checking method:** parsed the retrieved official XHTML, enumerated the publisher's own ids (`rct_1`–`rct_10`, `art_1`, `art_2`, `anx_1`) and every Annex list item and requirement code; confirmed the act's identity, adoption and publication dates and the Article 2 commencement clause against the retrieved text; checked that no amending/repeal relationship is stated. EUR-Lex ELI returned an empty HTTP 202 (bot protection), so the anchor scheme was verified against the Publications Office source instead; the ELI base URL is the standard EUR-Lex one.
- **External works not inspected:** ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1 (2025-02), ETSI EN 319 401, the ENISA "Methodology for sectoral cybersecurity assessments" and the cross-referenced acts (2015/1502, 765/2008, 2024/1183, 2016/679, 2018/1725, 2002/58/EC) themselves. Their full technical requirements are not silently imported here and need their own registers. No external-standard conformity claim follows from these rows.
- **Repository grounding:** E1–E6 show Autogram performs document signing and EU LOTL-based signature validation and supports eID cards as signing tokens; it contains no wallet, remote-onboarding, identity-proofing, face-image or biometric functionality, and is not a wallet provider, PID provider, IPSP or wallet-relying party. The requirements are therefore scoped out rather than assessed as completed or breached.
- **Outstanding evidence (only if a future role arises):** a Member-State mandate or wallet/onboarding arrangement, IPSP or PID-provider contracts, accredited conformity-assessment certificates, practice statement, risk assessment, FAR/FRR analysis, NFC/chip-authentication and reference-face-image test records, and a data-protection assessment for biometric processing. None is an established present missing statutory measure for Autogram.
- **Broader uncertainties not resolved here:** eIDAS Article 15 end-user-product classification, CRA commercial-supply/manufacturer status, and all-country signature-validity correctness are unaffected by this wallet-onboarding-specific exclusion. Repository evidence is narrow; no released binary, complete codebase role audit or organisational records were examined.
