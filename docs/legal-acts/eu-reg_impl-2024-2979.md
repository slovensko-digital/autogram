# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2979 — integrity and core functionalities of European Digital Identity Wallets

## Executive summary

This act lays down **wallet integrity and core-functionality** rules — wallet unit integrity, wallet secure cryptographic applications/devices, wallet unit attestations and revocation, data formats, transaction logs, embedded disclosure, qualified signatures, signature creation applications, data export and pseudonyms. Its addressees are **wallet providers, wallet units/instances and Member States** in the EUDI-Wallet ecosystem. Under the confirmed scope, Autogram is **not a wallet solution, wallet provider, wallet-relying party, PID/EAA issuer or trust-service provider**; it is a desktop and CLI application that signs and validates files ([README, lines 4–19](../../README.md#L4-L19)). The individual wallet duties below are therefore scoped out, not reported as completed or breached. A local signing application or a localhost API does not establish a wallet role.

The original act was adopted **28 November 2024**, published **4 December 2024** and entered into force **24 December 2024**. It is now amended by **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1731**, effective **11 August 2026**, which deletes Article 3(2) and Article 14(1), replaces parts of Articles 5, 6, 9, 10 and 12, inserts Articles 5a (cryptographic mechanisms) and 14a (EU Digital Identity Wallet Trust Mark), adds Annexes Ia/Ib/VI/VII/VIII and replaces or deletes Annexes II/III/V. Those amendment operations belong to the [amending-act register](eu-reg_impl-2026-1731.md); the fullest current text is the [11 August 2026 consolidation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R2979-20260811). None is an Autogram release deadline. No wallet-profile conformity, certification, issuance arrangement or wallet transaction-log deployment was audited. This is an engineering/legal reading aid, not legal advice or certification.

Rows cite **B**, **C** or **M** as defined above with the exact provision identifier. **S** means the requirement addresses a state, wallet, wallet-relying party or PID/EAA issuer absent from the confirmed E1–E7 scope. **F** names the evidence needed *if* a future deployment introduces that role: role/service contracts, national scheme/mandate, wallet certification, issuer and format/profile tests. Neither S nor F means a present compliance gap. Recitals and definitions are interpretative context rather than software tasks. Technical annex rows are None (or, for signature formats, Indirect) rather than blanket Direct: the app does not operate a wallet unit or consume/issue these wallet credentials in the established scope.
